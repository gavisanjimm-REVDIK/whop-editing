# whop-editing – standing instructions

## Every video gets a title and captions (always)
Whenever a video is generated or re-cut, also write its **title and captions** in the same turn, without being asked:
- Add or update a section for the file in the campaign's captions file (e.g. `R3BORN_CAPTIONS.md`, `CAPTIONS.md`).
- Include: **YouTube Shorts title** (under 40 characters before hashtags), **YouTube description**, and
  **TikTok** and **Instagram** captions (plus X if the campaign gives an X handle).
- Follow the campaign brief's rules: required tags/handles per platform, required hashtags, banned words/topics,
  no misleading claims (only things said or shown in the clip). Use 2–3 relevant hashtags.
- Paste the title + captions in the reply next to the delivered video, ready to copy.

## R3born (HardScope) campaign rules (from the brief)
- Tag: YouTube `@HardScope`, TikTok `@hardscope`, Instagram `@hardscope`; `#r3born` in every caption.
- Never mention FaZe anywhere; no politics; nothing that makes HardScope look bad.
- Episode 2 footage only, at least 7 s, big readable subtitles, never the full episode.
