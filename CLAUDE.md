# Working notes for this repo

## Every video delivery MUST include captions + YouTube title
Whenever a clip is rendered and sent, always also give (in the reply, ready to paste, and saved in the
campaign's CAPTIONS file):
1. **Instagram Reels / TikTok caption**
2. **YouTube Shorts title** (no hashtags in the title)
3. **YouTube Shorts description**

Rules for the current SEGA / STRANGER THAN HEAVEN campaign (`CAPTIONS_STH.md`):
- Caption text is copied **exactly** from the brief, never reworded:
  `Pre-order or Wishlist STRANGER THAN HEAVEN now for XBOX Series/Game Pass/Cloud/PC, PlayStation 5, or Steam and play on January 15th. @sth_game`
- YouTube description adds `@SEGA_West` after `@sth_game`.
- `#Ad` on its own line, first hashtag; then max 3 game-related hashtags, varied per video.
- Titles/hooks: engaging but true, make clear it's a video game, no comparisons to other games,
  no rage bait, never call trailer footage "gameplay".
