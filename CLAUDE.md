# whop-editing: standing rules

- **Every time you make a video, also write a YouTube Shorts title and caption for it.** Do this for every
  campaign, without being asked.
  - Put them in a `YOUTUBE_CAPTIONS.md` in that campaign's folder. Also paste them in the final chat reply,
    ready to copy.
  - For each video, give 2–3 title options (short hooks, under 100 characters, 1–2 emojis are fine), a
    description/caption, and a pinned comment.
  - Follow that campaign's brief for required tags, credits, disclosure (`#Ad`, AI label) and spelling. Use at
    most 3–4 hashtags and include `#shorts`.
  - List anything that has to be set when uploading (e.g. YouTube's "Altered or synthetic content" toggle).
